Before I knew 🌱

Because it means there's still so much we haven't done. So many pictures we haven't taken. So many places we haven't been. So many memories that don't exist yet.

This isn't a scrapbook of everything we've done.

A little note for you ♡

I honestly love the way you make me feel. Somehow, you can turn the most random, normal day into something I actually want to remember. And the funny thing is, we've already made so many memories together, yet I feel like we've barely even started. Some of my favourite memories have to be those late night talks we used to have staying up way later than we probably should've, talking about literally everything and absolutely nothing at the same time. And of course, playing What Do You Meme with you was genuinely one of my favourites. Who knew looking at random memes could become a core memory? 😭 But the best part is knowing that there are still so many memories waiting for us. Right now we're far from each other, and yeah, that kinda sucks sometimes, but I know this isn't where our story ends. One day we'll actually be together, travelling around the world, doing all the stupid little things we've talked about and making memories we haven't even imagined yet. And Japan... yeah, we're definitely going to Japan. Imagine us actually being there after all the times we've talked about it. Walking around together, exploring places we've only seen on screens, taking a million pictures, getting lost because neither of us knows where we're going basically the perfect disaster. 😭 There's still so much I want to experience with you. More late night talks, more games, more random conversations, more trips, more laughs, more memories, and probably a lot more arguments over absolutely stupid things too lolol We've already got some beautiful memories behind us, but honestly, I'm way more excited about the ones we haven't made yet. Because if the memories we've made so far mean this much to me, I can't even imagine how special the ones waiting for us will be. So yeah, we're far apart right now. But someday, we'll look back at this distance and laugh about how we somehow managed to make it work.

— from someone who adores you ♡
