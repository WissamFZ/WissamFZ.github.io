I honestly love the way you make me feel. Somehow, you can turn the most random, normal day into something I actually want to remember. And the funny thing is, we've already made so many memories together, yet I feel like we've barely even started. And of course, playing What Do You Meme with you was genuinely one of my favourites. And Japan... yeah, we're definitely going to Japan. Imagine us actually being there after all the times we've talked about it. Walking around together, exploring places we've only seen on screens, taking a million pictures, getting lost because neither of us knows where we're going basically the perfect disaster. 😭 There's still so much I want to experience with you. More late night talks, more games, more random conversations, more trips, more laughs, more memories, and probably a lot more arguments over absolutely stupid things too lolol We've already got some beautiful memories behind us, but honestly, I'm way more excited about the ones we haven't made yet. Because if the memories we've made so far mean this much to me, I can't even imagine how special the ones waiting for us will be. So yeah, we're far apart right now. But someday, we'll look back at this distance and laugh about how we somehow managed to make it work.
